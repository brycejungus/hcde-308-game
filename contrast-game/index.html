How to play

Every round you get one color to hunt for. Here’s what the game screen looks like:

  1. 1Study the color in the gray box. That’s the one you’re hunting for.
  2. 2Click every tile on the board that’s exactly that color. Click a tile again to unselect it.
  3. 3Press Check before time runs out. Find every match with no wrong picks to pass the round. Pass 3 of 5 to clear the level.
  4. 4Remove backgrounds afterward to see every tile on plain gray — and find out how you were fooled.

Heads up: the background changes how a color looks. The same blue looks lighter on a dark background and darker on a light one. Trust the swatch, not your surroundings.
